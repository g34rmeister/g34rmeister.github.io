1.3 About RISE Bot Instructor

Gerald Lê Draft

01. The project

RISE Bot Instructor is an online, web-based platform that teaches students how to program robots, with a focus on industrial robotics. It is being built by Gerald Lê as an undergraduate honors independent research project (CSC 3991) in the Department of Computer Science at Louisiana State University in Spring 2026, advised by Felipe Fronchetti.

The goal is a site that a student can work through on their own: short, readable articles that start from first principles and build toward how real industrial robots are programmed.

02. Who it is for

The intended reader is a student who can already program a little but has not worked with robots. No robotics hardware is required to follow along, although the examples in Chapter 2 use an Arduino, a common low-cost board, so readers who have one can try them.

03. What it covers

The project proposal calls for learning modules on robot types, applications, programming modes (teach pendant, offline and block-based programming) and safety concepts, supported by diagrams and lightweight interactive elements. The course is organized into three chapters:

  • Chapter 1: Introduction: what robots are and how to use this course.
  • Chapter 2: Electronics and Control Basics: Arduino, PWM, encoders and PID control.
  • Chapter 3: Programming Industrial Robots: online and offline programming, teach pendants and PolyScope 5, ABB RobotStudio, and the URScript and RAPID languages.

04. Project plan

The proposal splits the semester into six stages:

1–2Establish project scope, content and learning goalsProposal and site outline: audience, learning objectives, sitemap and topic list
3–4Define visual identity and interaction modelLow-fidelity wireframes and interaction flows
5–7Implement the core website framework and navigationResponsive site skeleton with navigation and base styling
8–10Develop educational content on industrial roboticsModules on robot types, applications, programming modes and safety
11–12Integrate visual explanations and learning aidsDiagrams, animations and simple interactive elements
13–15Refine, validate and present the final websiteUsability refinements, content polish, basic user testing and a final presentation

05. What comes next

Early feedback on the project asked for a narrower, less repetitive scope, more hands-on coding and building, more visual polish and photographs, and an interactive quiz feature. Those are the next steps for the site.
