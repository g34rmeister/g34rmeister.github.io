      Chapter 1: Intro to Robotics

      Gerald Lê

      01. Overview

      Before you program a robot, it helps to know what counts as one, where robots came from and what kinds are in use today. These lessons give you that common ground for the rest of the course.

      02. What you will learn

      • Definitions: what separates a robot from an ordinary machine.
      • History: how automation evolved into modern robots.
      • Types: industrial robots, cobots, mobile robots, SCARA robots and AGVs.
      • Prerequisites: the programming, math and mindset that make robotics easier.
      • Applications: production, human interaction, exploration and safety.
      • Anatomy: joints, degrees of freedom, coordinate frames and how an arm moves between points.

      03. Articles in this chapter

      1. 1.1 Definition of Robots: What a robot is, where the word came from, and the sense, think, act loop every robot shares.
      2. 1.2 History and Evolution of Robots: How automation evolved over time, from early attempts at automatic machines to modern automation.
      3. 1.3 Types of Robots: The main types of robots: industrial arms, cobots, mobile robots, SCARA robots and autonomous guided vehicles.
      4. 1.4 Preliminary Requirements: The skills worth having before starting robotics: Python, C++, some math, the right learning mentality, assembly language and networking basics.
      5. 1.5 Common Applications of Robotics: Where robots are used: in production, in interaction with people, in scientific exploration and in protecting human lives.
      6. 1.6 Robot Anatomy and Coordinate Frames: How an industrial arm is built: joints, degrees of freedom, the work envelope, coordinate frames, the tool center point, and how a controller interpolates joint and straight-line moves.
      7. 1.7 The Industrial Robot Cell: What turns a robot arm into a working cell: components, safeguarding and light-curtain distance, PLC-robot handshakes, integrator roles with FAT and SAT, three typical cells, cycle time and cost.
