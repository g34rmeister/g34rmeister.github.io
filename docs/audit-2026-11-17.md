3. **Very long lessons with few quiz questions.** 4-9, 2-5, 5-4 have 3,877/3,802/3,836 words and 3 questions; every lesson over 3,400 words (1-7, 2-5..2-8, 4-7..4-9, 5-4, 5-5, 6-9, 6-10, 11-1..11-3) has 3-4. Fix: split the longest (4-9, 11-3, 5-4) or add 2 questions each; append new lessons only (URLs stay stable), so splitting means a new lesson id.
4. **Interactive elements are uneven.** 37 of 70 lessons have no widget; Ch5, Ch10, Ch11 have none; Ch3 has 1 of 5. 8 lessons have no Try it: 3-1..3-5, 8-4, 9-2, 11-4. 4-10 and 10-5 (both labs) have no figure. Fix: one Try it in each Ch3 lesson (contribute-a-fix tasks), widgets in Ch5 (risk score, safety distance S=K*T+C).
5. **Data hygiene.** 38 key terms are not found verbatim in their lesson text (section 4), 0 lack glossary entries. 22 of 70 lessons have partial refs (36 refs) and 48 refs are unreachable across the course, mostly Ch1, 2, 6 (6-6: 4, 6-1: 4, 1-1: 4, 2-1: 4). Fix: re-check or replace unreachable refs; rename the key terms or add the term to the text.
