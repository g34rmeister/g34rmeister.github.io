Vivan

CSC 4330 Project
Full-Stack Language Learning Platform · Fall 2025

A web app for learning Louisiana Creole (Kouri-Vini), an endangered language of Louisiana, since expanded to other subjects. Built by a team of five LSU CS undergraduates for CSC 4330 (Software Systems Development) under Dr. Felipe Fronchetti.

  • Learners work through predefined courses or build and share their own in a "Room Editor" made of question, text, blank, and media components.
  • Collaborative course authoring, public/private course visibility, streaks, and badges.
  • Security features including password hashing, optional two-factor authentication, audit logs, brute-force prevention, and profanity filtering.
React Tailwind / shadcn Django Team of 5
