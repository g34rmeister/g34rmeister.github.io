TigerBot Autonomous Robotics Fleet

Honors Thesis
Multi-Modal Autonomous Systems & Embedded Kinematics

Undergraduate Honors Engineering Thesis research at Louisiana State University investigating 2D/3D LiDAR SLAM, closed-loop 6-DOF manipulator inverse kinematics, deterministic flight avionics, and quadruped locomotion across heterogeneous embedded compute tiers.

Key Research & Architectural Focus:

  • Real-time indoor/outdoor SLAM using Cartographer and costmap-based Nav2 autonomous waypoint navigation.
  • Dual-solver 6-DOF manipulator inverse kinematics via Trac-IK with sub-0.8 ms convergence and YOLOv8 6D pose grasp estimation.
  • Heterogeneous embedded compute architecture pairing NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano with real-time STM32 microcontrollers over Micro-ROS.
