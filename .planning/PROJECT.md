# Project Overview

**Project:** Gerald Lê — Portfolio & Robotics Thesis Platform  
**Owner:** Gerald Lê (`g34rmeister`)  
**Affiliation:** Computer Science @ Louisiana State University (LSU)  
**Live Site:** https://g34rmeister.github.io  

---

## Mission
Provide a fast, responsive, and accessible personal portfolio showcasing academic research, software engineering projects, and an interactive master's thesis hub dedicated to autonomous robotics.

---

## Primary Pillars
1. **Academic Portfolio:** Showcase research publications, industry experience, and software engineering projects with rich filtering and clean typography.
2. **Robotics Thesis Platform:** Interactive platform presenting research on autonomous ground vehicles (AGV), UAV flight, quadruped locomotion, and robotic arm manipulation (SO-100).
3. **Performance & Privacy:** Zero-dependency static site architecture, 100% locally hosted assets, dark/light theme persistence, and lightning-fast load times.
