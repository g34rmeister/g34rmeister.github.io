RISE Bot Instructor

RISE Bot Instructor is a web-based course that teaches students how to program robots, with a focus on industrial robotics. It starts with what a robot is, builds up the electronics and control ideas behind every moving machine (PWM, encoders and PID control), and then shows how industrial arms are programmed in practice: teach pendants, offline simulation, and vendor languages like URScript and RAPID.
